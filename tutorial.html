Step 01 of 12
What is AURUM?
AURUM is a private market intelligence and paper-trading system — not a screener, not a generic trading bot. A funnel screens the market for assets the algorithms can actually read. The Governor runs your algorithm suite on each one and combines the results into a single composite score. A gate decides what is worth trading, and a position monitor manages every open trade around the clock.

AURUM works in two markets — Stocks and Crypto — toggled on the Brief page. Scoring can run on pure arithmetic (Deterministic, free) or with Claude reviewing each candidate.

Everything is measured. Every scored candidate is recorded, whether it opened or not, so the system is judged on evidence rather than on any one good week.
Always On
The Position Monitor runs server-side whether the app is open or not — trailing stops, timeouts, stops and targets, in both directions. Open a position, close the app, and AURUM keeps managing it.

Step 05 of 12
The Simulator
The Simulator trades $10,000 of simulated capital — no broker, no real money. It is where AURUM actually runs: scanning, opening positions and managing them. The controls, top to bottom:
MODE
Scoring mode — DETERMINISTIC is free and the recommended default. CLAUDE adds a model review of each candidate at a cost per call. BOTH runs Claude and records the deterministic score beside it, to compare the two.
SIZE
Scale preset — Conservative: 10 positions at 5%. Scaled: 30 at 3%. Aggressive: 50 at 2%. A bigger preset usually opens the same few names at a smaller size, so Conservative is the default — and the one to use for Swing mode.
SWING
Swing mode — Off: longs only. On: ten longs and ten shorts, with reversals. It has its own step later.
SCAN
FILL TO 10 ONCE / AUTONOMOUS — Fill runs one scan now. Autonomous keeps the book filled while the app is open, rescanning after a position closes or once an hour.
LOG
Scan log — The last 80 lines, newest first: what was screened, what sat out, how each wave scored and why candidates failed. Read it whenever a scan opens fewer positions than you expect.
COPY
Copy All — Copies every open position as plain text: entry, stop, target, trailing status, volatility and more.
CHECK
System Diagnostics — SCAN checks your live data. RECALIBRATE rebuilds derived figures. CHECK OUTCOMES resolves results for scored candidates.

Step 06 of 12
How Picks Are Chosen
Most of the market is noise to the algorithms. Before a single score is computed, the funnel narrows it to what they can read.
BAND
The volatility band — Only coins moving 1% to 4.5% a day, ranked toward 3%. In your own data, coins in the 2.5-3.5% range won about 70% of the time; coins moving more than 6% a day won 14-17%.
SIZE
Size and liquidity — Market cap above $150M and daily volume above $25M, so there is a real price to trade at.
SKIP
What never enters — Stablecoins, tokenized stocks, gold tokens, and tokenized loans or funds. They look calm because they are pegged to something else.
LIST
Your watchlist — Competes on the same score as everything else, and sits out any scan where it is too volatile. The scan log names what sat out.
GATE
The gate — After scoring, three tests must pass: signal quality, direction and opportunity. Most candidates land near the middle, so most scans reject more than they open. That is the system being selective, not broken.

Step 07 of 12
How Trades Are Managed
Once a position opens, the Position Monitor takes over — every few minutes, server-side, whether the app is open or not.
STOP
Stop and target — Set from the coin’s own volatility: the stop about 2x its daily range away, the target at 2:1.
TRAIL
Trailing stop — Once a position is up half a day’s movement, its stop moves to breakeven, then follows the price one day’s movement behind. A winner can no longer become a loser.
STALE
Stale rule — A swing position that moves under 3% in 7 days closes, unless it is in profit. Anything still open at two weeks closes regardless.
PRICE
Pricing — From the exact CoinGecko id stored on each position, with a second provider as backup. A backup price that looks like a different coin is rejected rather than risk a false stop.
Why this matters
Before the trailing stop, ten positions climbed to +$120 together, then the market turned and gave all of it back. Nothing was due to close, so the system held every winner all the way down. The trailing stop exists so that cannot happen again.

Step 08 of 12
Swing Mode
Swing mode trades both directions — longs predicted to rise, shorts predicted to fall. Turn it on with SWING on the Simulator; turn it off and everything runs exactly as before.
LONG
Longs on a bullish read — The composite must read bullish. Each side trades only what genuinely qualifies, so the book follows the market rather than being forced into balance.
SHORT
Shorts on a confirmed downtrend — Price below its 50-day average, the 20-day below the 50-day and falling, a lower high. Best entry: the price rallying back toward its 20-day average. Never oversold, never mid-crash.
SIZE
Sized by risk — Each position is sized to its volatility, so a stop costs the same on a calm coin as on a jumpy one.
STALL
Reversal at a stall — Nothing about reversing is decided at entry. Once a Swing position has captured capital and its trailing stop stops moving for 8 hours, it is checked for a turn.
TURN
Only a confirmed turn — It reverses only when the old trend has broken, not on a pullback. Tested on 96 pullbacks and bounces: zero false reversals. An original stop hit at a loss never reverses.
Paper only
Your wallet trades on a spot exchange, which cannot short, so Swing mode refuses to run while live trading is armed. Only positions opened in Swing mode can ever be reversed. The downtrend rule is standard practice but untested on your account, so treat the first runs as data.

The Trading, Options & Crypto Pages
When you're ready to act on real signals, the Trading page generates live Governor-backed instructions for both stocks and crypto. The Options page assesses your open options positions.
MODE
Manual or Auto — Manual shows five ranked trades to pick from. Auto autofills a single highest-conviction trade, ready to execute.
TYPE
Stock or Options, Day or Swing — Choose your instrument and time horizon before generating an instruction.
CRYPTO
Crypto Mode — Toggle STOCKS / CRYPTO in the Brief page header. In crypto mode, the Governor swaps in 6 crypto-specific algorithms. Claude generates DEX execution instructions — Uniswap, dYdX, or Jupiter. Both LONG and SHORT supported.
DEX
No Broker, No ID Required — Crypto trades execute via decentralized exchanges. Connect a MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet and follow Claude's exact execution note. No account, no KYC.
WALLET
Connecting Your Wallet — In Auto mode, tap Connect Wallet for DEX Execution in the auto-fill panel. AURUM detects MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet automatically. Your shortened address appears on the button when connected. Keep funds in USDC or ETH for Uniswap trades, USDC for dYdX perpetuals.
MONITOR
Position Monitor — Once a trade is open, close the app. The Position Monitor (quick-service) manages it server-side: trailing stop, stale rule, stop and target. Stocks and crypto, long and short, while you are completely offline.
ASSESS
Options Assessment — For open options positions, Claude returns TAKE PROFIT, HOLD, FLIP, or EXIT based on current signal alignment and days to expiration.

The Self-Rating System
This is what separates AURUM from a generic stock tool. The self-rating system logs every pick at the moment it's made — then grades AURUM's accuracy automatically 30 days later against real market performance.
LOG
Every pick is logged — Ticker, score, brief, entry price, and timestamp. Nothing is cherry-picked after the fact.
GRADE
Automatic grading — 30 days after each pick, AURUM compares its return against the S&P 500 (SPY) over the same window. This runs quietly in the background whenever you open the Admin page.
REPORT
Monthly Report — The Report page shows pick accuracy, simulator P&L, and lets you download a full PDF summary for any month.
TRUST
Verified track record — The accuracy percentage shown is real — earned over time, not projected. That's what makes it trustworthy.
SHADOW
Every scored candidate is recorded — Opened or not, with its scores, direction, volatility and source. That is what calibration reads, so the data builds from scans, not from how many positions you hold.
DIAG
The Diagnostic page — Checks the whole system end to end. When something looks wrong, run its Workers, Governor, Live code and Workflow sections.
