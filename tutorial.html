What is AURUM?
AURUM is a private stock intelligence system — not a trading app, not a screener. It thinks, watches, scores, and grades itself over time.

Most stock apps give you charts and price alerts. AURUM gives you synthesized intelligence — pulling from news, political signals, economic data, insider activity, and options flow — then running it through 11 custom algorithms to produce a formal briefing on each stock you're watching.

The self-rating system is what makes AURUM unique. Every pick is logged with its entry price. Over 30, 60, and 90 days AURUM grades its own accuracy. That track record is the product.
The Core Principle
"Intelligence. Refined." — AURUM doesn't guess. It synthesizes, scores, and proves itself over time.

WATCHLIST
Watchlist — Every stock AURUM is actively monitoring. Added through the Admin panel on your instruction.

The Simulator
The Paper Trading Simulator lets you test AURUM's picks with simulated money before committing real capital. Every position tracks real gain and loss based on prices you enter. The portfolio grades itself over time.

TRACK

CLOSE

RESET
Reset Anytime — Start fresh with $10,000 whenever you want. Previous simulation data is cleared cleanly.

The Self-Rating System
This is what separates AURUM from every other stock tool. The self-rating system logs every pick at the moment it's made — then grades AURUM's accuracy 30, 60, and 90 days later against actual market performance.
LOG
Every pick is logged — Ticker, score, brief, entry price, and timestamp. Nothing is cherry-picked after the fact.
GRADE
Automatic grading — At 30, 60, and 90 days AURUM compares pick performance against the S&P 500. Did it beat the market or not?
LEARN
System improves — After 6-12 months of data the self-rating reveals which signals predicted correctly. Algorithms get refined based on real evidence.
TRUST
Verified track record — The accuracy percentage on the Brief page is real — earned over time, not projected. That's what makes it trustworthy.
