Step 01 of 09
What is AURUM?
AURUM is a private market intelligence system — not a screener, not a generic trading bot. It runs 25 algorithms across price action, sentiment, options flow, insider activity, and macro conditions, then synthesizes them through a master Governor before Claude turns the result into a plain-language trade instruction.

Every signal you see — composite score, verdict, signal quality — traces back to real algorithm output, not a black box. You can test everything risk-free in the Simulator, or generate live trade instructions on the Trading page.

The self-rating system is what makes AURUM accountable. Every pick is logged with its entry price and automatically graded against the S&P 500, 30 days later. That track record is the product.
The Core Principle
"Intelligence. Refined." — AURUM doesn't guess. It synthesizes, scores, and proves itself over time.

Step 06 of 09
The Trading, Options & Crypto Pages
When you're ready to act on real signals, the Trading page generates live Governor-backed instructions for both stocks and crypto. The Options page assesses your open options positions.
MODE
Manual or Auto — Manual shows five ranked trades to pick from. Auto autofills a single highest-conviction trade, ready to execute.
TYPE
Stock or Options, Day or Swing — Choose your instrument and time horizon before generating an instruction.
CRYPTO
Crypto Mode — Toggle STOCKS / CRYPTO in the Brief page header. In crypto mode, the Governor swaps in 6 crypto-specific algorithms. Claude generates DEX execution instructions — Uniswap, dYdX, or Jupiter. Both LONG and SHORT supported.
DEX
No Broker, No ID Required — Crypto trades execute via decentralized exchanges. Connect a MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet and follow Claude's exact execution note. No account, no KYC.
ASSESS
Options Assessment — For open options positions, Claude returns TAKE PROFIT, HOLD, FLIP, or EXIT based on current signal alignment and days to expiration.
Crypto Free Tier
Crypto mode runs at ~70-75% capacity on free tier using Binance funding rates, CoinGecko, Alternative.me Fear & Greed, and Claude supplemental estimates. Coinglass ($29/mo) pushes it to ~85%.

The Admin Panel
The Admin panel is AURUM's control center. You manage your watchlist, improve algorithms in plain English, set price alerts, and run a system health scan — all from one place.

Manage Watchlist — Add stocks to AURUM's monitoring universe. These tickers power the Brief, Trading, and Sectors pages.

ALERT
Price Alerts — Set alerts for when a stock moves up or down beyond your threshold.
SCAN

Important
Add stocks deliberately, not arbitrarily. A focused watchlist produces sharper, more useful intelligence than a sprawling one.

Step 08 of 09
The Self-Rating System
This is what separates AURUM from a generic stock tool. The self-rating system logs every pick at the moment it's made — then grades AURUM's accuracy automatically 30 days later against real market performance.
LOG
Every pick is logged — Ticker, score, brief, entry price, and timestamp. Nothing is cherry-picked after the fact.
GRADE
Automatic grading — 30 days after each pick, AURUM compares its return against the S&P 500 (SPY) over the same window. This runs quietly in the background whenever you open the Admin page.
REPORT
Monthly Report — The Report page shows pick accuracy, simulator P&L, and lets you download a full PDF summary for any month.
TRUST
Verified track record — The accuracy percentage shown is real — earned over time, not projected. That's what makes it trustworthy.
