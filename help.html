General Questions
What exactly is AURUM?
+
AURUM is a private market intelligence system. It aggregates news, economic data, political signals, and sentiment — runs it through 11 custom algorithms — and produces a formal intelligence brief on stocks you're watching. It doesn't trade. It thinks, scores, and grades itself over time.
When does AURUM generate picks?
+
Picks are generated every Monday at 9am automatically. The weekly analysis runs all 11 algorithms against your watchlist and produces scored picks with formal briefs. You don't need to do anything — it happens without you.
When do I receive the weekly brief email?
+
Every Monday at 10am AURUM emails a full intelligence brief to your registered email. It includes current picks, system accuracy, watchlist, and top news headlines — formatted in AURUM's luxury aesthetic.
What does the pick score mean?
+
The score (0-100) represents AURUM's composite confidence in a stock based on all active signal inputs. A score of 80+ means strong alignment across multiple signals. 60-79 means moderate alignment. Below 60 means AURUM is watching but not high conviction. The score alone is not a buy signal — read the brief and probability spread too.
How does AURUM grade its own accuracy?
+
Every pick is logged at the moment it's made with the entry price and timestamp. At 30, 60, and 90 days AURUM compares the pick's performance against the S&P 500. If the pick outperformed the market it's marked as a win. The accuracy percentage on the Brief page reflects this verified track record — not projections.
What does AURUM cost to run?
+
At current scale AURUM runs for approximately $15/month — almost entirely Claude API usage for the weekly analysis. News fetching uses free APIs. Hosting is free via GitHub Pages. Supabase database is free tier. Email is free via Resend. The cost scales up only when you add paid data sources like Benzinga or Bloomberg.
The Algorithms
What does the Momentum Detector do?
+
Measures price velocity over the last 14 days combined with volume trends. A stock moving up fast with increasing volume scores higher than one drifting up on thin volume. Flags strong directional movement candidates for AURUM's analysis cycle.
What does the Sentiment Detector do?
+
Combines news sentiment (60% weight) and social sentiment (40% weight) into a single score. Returns bullish, bearish, or neutral signal. News is weighted more heavily because it reflects institutional awareness while social reflects retail momentum.
What does the Political Signal Detector do?
+
Tracks congressional trading activity and policy event signals. When members of Congress are buying a stock that's a meaningful signal — they often have advance knowledge of favorable legislation. Adjusts score up when congressional buying exceeds selling, down when the reverse is true.
What does the Options Flow Detector do?
+
Detects unusual options activity — when call or put volume significantly exceeds historical averages. This is often smart money positioning before a major price move. Weighted 2x in the probability spread model because it's one of the most reliable leading indicators available.
What does the Insider Transaction Tracker do?
+
Tracks SEC Form 4 filings — when executives buy or sell their own company stock with personal funds. CEO and CFO purchases are weighted heavily because insiders rarely buy their own stock unless they believe it's undervalued. Weighted 2x in the probability spread model alongside options flow.
What does the Macro Regime Detector do?
+
Identifies the current macroeconomic regime — high rate growth, low rate growth, stagflation risk, recession, or yield curve inversion — and applies sector-specific adjustments. In a high rate environment finance scores higher and tech scores lower. In recession healthcare and utilities outperform. AURUM adjusts automatically.
What is the Probability Spread Model?
+
AURUM's master intelligence layer. It synthesizes all 10 signal outputs — applying smart money weighting, macro regime adjustments, earnings cycle penalties, and sentiment velocity — into a full probability distribution. Produces bull case, base case, and bear case return estimates with probability percentages and a confidence rating. Think of it like a point spread for stocks.
How do I improve an algorithm?
+
Go to the Admin panel → Algorithm Improvement section. Select the algorithm you want to change. Describe the improvement in plain English — for example "The momentum detector flags too many small cap stocks. I only want large cap above $10B market cap." AURUM rewrites the code and presents it for your approval before anything deploys. You never need to touch code directly.
Features & Pages
How do I add stocks to the watchlist?
+
Go to Admin → Manage Watchlist. Enter the ticker, company name, and sector. Only add stocks when advised — either by AURUM's analysis or by Claude directly. Every stock added gets analyzed weekly by all 11 algorithms. Quality over quantity — a focused watchlist produces sharper intelligence.
How does the Paper Trading Simulator work?
+
The Simulator gives you $10,000 in simulated capital. Open positions by selecting a watchlist stock, entering your entry price and number of shares. Update the current price anytime to see real gain or loss calculations. Close positions when done. The portfolio tracks your total return over time — letting you validate AURUM's picks before committing real money.
How does the News Feed work?
+
AURUM automatically fetches news every morning at 8am from Yahoo Finance, Google News, Reddit, SEC EDGAR, and Alpha Vantage. Each headline is scored for sentiment and cross-referenced with your watchlist. You can also tap Fetch Now on the News page for an immediate refresh. Paid sources (Bloomberg, Reuters, Benzinga) activate when API keys are added.
How do Price Alerts work?
+
Go to Admin → Price Alerts. Select a watchlist stock, choose whether you want to alert on upward or downward movement, set your percentage threshold, and enter your entry price. AURUM monitors and flags when the threshold is reached. Alerts are visible in the Admin panel and can be removed anytime.
What does the Sectors page show?
+
The Sectors page groups your watchlist stocks by sector — Technology, Finance, Energy, etc. — and shows the average AURUM score per sector. This helps you see where AURUM's collective intelligence is most bullish and whether your watchlist is too concentrated in one sector. The score bar shows relative strength visually.
What is the Pick History Archive?
+
The History page logs every pick AURUM has ever made — score, brief, signal breakdown, date, and outcome. Filter by All, Real, Simulated, or Score 80+. This is your verified track record — the foundation of AURUM's credibility over time. Nothing is deleted or hidden.
Glossary of Terms
Composite Score
AURUM's 0-100 rating for a stock. Synthesizes all active signal inputs into a single number representing overall conviction.
Probability Spread
Bull case, base case, and bear case return estimates with probability percentages. Modeled after how professional traders think about position risk.
Signal Conflict
When AURUM's algorithms disagree significantly — spread of 35+ points between highest and lowest signal. Reduces confidence rating to Conflicted.
Smart Money
Institutional investors, hedge funds, and corporate insiders whose positioning often predicts price movement. Options flow and insider transactions are AURUM's smart money signals.
Macro Regime
The current macroeconomic environment — high rate growth, low rate growth, stagflation risk, recession, or yield curve inversion. Different regimes favor different sectors.
Sentiment Velocity
The rate of change in sentiment — not just whether it's positive but whether it's accelerating or decelerating. Accelerating positive sentiment is a stronger signal than peaked sentiment.
Self-Rating
AURUM's system for grading its own accuracy. Picks are logged at entry price and compared against actual performance at 30, 60, and 90 days.
Beat Market
A pick that outperformed the S&P 500 return over the same period. The primary benchmark for AURUM's self-rating system.
Correlation Risk
When two or more watchlist stocks move almost identically. High correlation means your watchlist provides less diversification than it appears.
Earnings Cycle
The period around a company's quarterly earnings report. AURUM applies a volatility penalty to picks within 7-14 days of earnings due to elevated uncertainty.
Options Flow
The volume and direction of options contracts being traded. Unusual call volume suggests bullish smart money positioning. Unusual put volume suggests bearish positioning.
Form 4
SEC filing required when corporate insiders buy or sell their own company stock. AURUM monitors these filings via EDGAR as part of the insider transaction tracker.
Paper Trading
Simulated trading with virtual money. Used to test AURUM's picks without risking real capital. AURUM's simulator starts with $10,000 in simulated funds.
Tier 1 Source
Highest quality news sources — Yahoo Finance, Alpha Vantage, SEC EDGAR, Bloomberg, Reuters. Weighted more heavily in sentiment analysis.
Plug and Play
AURUM's architecture for paid data sources. The endpoint is already built — adding an API key activates the source immediately without any additional coding.
