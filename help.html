General Questions
What exactly is AURUM?
+
AURUM is a private market intelligence system. It runs 25 algorithms — momentum, sentiment, options flow, insider activity, macro regime, and more — through a master Governor that synthesizes every signal into a single composite score. Claude then turns that into a plain-language trade instruction. You can test everything in the Simulator with paper money, or generate trade instructions on the Trading page in Manual or Auto mode.
When does AURUM generate picks?
+
On demand. There is currently no automatic schedule — picks and trade instructions are generated whenever you tap Get Claude Trade Suggestion on the Simulator page, or Generate Instruction on the Trading page. Each time, the Governor runs all 25 algorithms fresh against live market data before Claude responds.
Do I get a weekly email brief?
+
Not currently. AURUM does not send emails. All intelligence lives inside the app — check the Brief page for your current picks, watchlist, and accuracy stats anytime.
What does the composite score mean?
+
The score (0-100) is the Governor's composite signal after weighting all 25 algorithms by reliability and current market regime. A score of 72+ signals STRONG_BUY conviction. 62-71 is BUY. 55-61 is WATCH_LONG. Below that, signals weaken toward HOLD, WATCH_SHORT, SELL, or STRONG_SELL. Each result also carries a signal quality score — if algorithms disagree sharply or key data sources are missing, quality drops and Claude is instructed to lower its confidence.
How does AURUM grade its own accuracy?
+
Every pick is logged at the moment it's made. 30 days later, AURUM automatically compares the pick's actual return against the S&P 500 (SPY) over the same window — this runs quietly in the background whenever you open the Admin page. If the pick beat SPY, it's marked a win. The accuracy percentage on the Report page reflects this verified track record.
What does AURUM cost to run?
+
Market data runs on Twelve Data and Finnhub free tiers. Hosting is free via GitHub Pages. The database runs on Supabase's free tier. The only real cost is Claude API usage, billed pay-as-you-go through Anthropic — typically a few dollars depending on how often you generate trade instructions.
Do I need a broker account to use AURUM?
+
No. The Simulator works entirely with paper money tracked in your own database — no broker, no ID verification, no signup required. A live broker connection (currently Alpaca, on the Brokerage page) is only needed if you eventually want AURUM to place real trades automatically. Until then, you can manually execute any Claude-suggested trade through whatever brokerage app you already use.
The Algorithms
How many algorithms does AURUM run, and what's the Governor?
+
AURUM runs 25 algorithms across four phases — Core Signals, Advanced Analysis, Evolutionary ML, and Deep AI — plus a 26th: the Governor (algorithm_orchestrator). The Governor runs last, weighting every algorithm's output by historical accuracy and current market regime, resolving conflicts, and producing one composite score before Claude ever sees the data.
What does the Momentum Detector do?
+
Measures price velocity using RSI, MACD, and rate-of-change across multiple timeframes, confirmed by volume trends. A stock moving up fast with rising volume scores higher than one drifting up on thin volume.
What does the Sentiment Detector do?
+
Analyzes recent news headlines from Finnhub for positive and negative language, combined with analyst recommendation data, to produce a bullish, bearish, or neutral sentiment score.
What does the Political Signal Detector do?
+
Tracks congressional trading activity when connected to a data source. Members of Congress buying a stock is treated as a meaningful bullish signal. This algorithm runs at neutral until a congressional trading data key is added.
What does the Options Flow Detector do?
+
Detects unusual options activity — sweeps, dark pool flow, and call/put volume imbalances. This is AURUM's highest-weighted algorithm in the Governor (2.2x) because options flow is one of the most reliable early signals of institutional positioning. Runs at neutral until an options flow data source is connected.
What does the Insider Transaction Tracker do?
+
Tracks insider buying and selling via Finnhub. Executives purchasing their own company's stock with personal funds is weighted heavily (2.0x in the Governor) — insiders rarely buy unless they believe the stock is undervalued.
What does the Macro Regime Detector do?
+
Identifies the current macroeconomic regime — goldilocks, low-rate expansion, high-rate expansion, recession, stagflation, risk-off, and more — and tells the Governor which algorithms to trust most in that environment. In a recession, macro and correlation signals get amplified; momentum gets dampened.
What is the Governor's signal quality score?
+
A 0-100 meta-score reflecting how much to trust a given run. It factors in how many of the 25 algorithms actually had real data to work with, how much they agreed with each other, and whether high-impact overrides (like an earnings date warning) were triggered. Claude is instructed to lower its confidence whenever signal quality drops below 60.
How do I improve an algorithm?
+
Go to the Admin panel → Algorithm Improvement section. Select the algorithm you want to change. Describe the improvement in plain English — for example "The momentum detector flags too many small cap stocks. I only want large cap above $10B market cap." AURUM rewrites the code and presents it for your approval before anything deploys. You never need to touch code directly.
Features & Pages
How do I add stocks to the watchlist?
+
Go to Admin → Manage Watchlist. Enter the ticker, company name, and sector. Watchlist tickers feed the price bar on the Brief and Trading pages, and are available as targets when generating a Governor-backed trade instruction. Quality over quantity — a focused watchlist produces sharper intelligence.
How does the Paper Trading Simulator work?
+
The Simulator gives you $10,000 in simulated capital. Tap Get Claude Trade Suggestion to have the Governor run all 25 algorithms and have Claude propose a complete trade — ticker, entry, stop loss, target, and reasoning. Hit Apply to Trade Form to autofill every field, or enter a trade manually. Update prices and close positions to track real gain/loss. No broker account is required — this is the recommended place to validate AURUM's signals before trading real money.
How does the News Feed work?
+
The News page displays headlines stored in AURUM's database, scored for sentiment and cross-referenced with your watchlist. Sentiment scoring for individual tickers is generated live by the Sentiment Detector algorithm whenever the Governor runs, using real-time headlines pulled from Finnhub.
How do Price Alerts work?
+
Go to Admin → Price Alerts. Select a watchlist stock, choose whether you want to alert on upward or downward movement, set your percentage threshold, and enter your entry price. AURUM monitors and flags when the threshold is reached. Alerts are visible in the Admin panel and can be removed anytime.
What does the Sectors page show?
+
The Sectors page groups your watchlist stocks by sector — Technology, Finance, Energy, etc. — and shows the average AURUM score per sector. This helps you see where AURUM's collective intelligence is most bullish and whether your watchlist is too concentrated in one sector. The score bar shows relative strength visually.
How does the Trading page work?
+
Choose Stock or Options, Day or Swing strategy, and Manual or Auto mode. Tap Generate Instruction — the Governor runs all 25 algorithms with live data, then Claude returns a complete trade instruction. Manual mode shows five ranked trades to choose from. Auto mode autofills a single highest-conviction trade, ready to execute through your connected broker.
What does the Options page do?
+
Tracks open options positions and gives Claude-powered assessments — TAKE PROFIT, HOLD, FLIP, or EXIT — based on current price, days to expiration, and AURUM's latest signal alignment for that ticker.
What is the Pick History Archive?
+
The History page logs every pick AURUM has ever made — score, brief, signal breakdown, date, and outcome. Filter by All, Real, Simulated, or Score 80+. This is your verified track record — the foundation of AURUM's credibility over time. Nothing is deleted or hidden.
Glossary of Terms
Composite Score
The Governor's 0-100 rating for a stock after weighting all 25 algorithms by reliability and market regime. Drives the verdict (STRONG_BUY through STRONG_SELL) and recommended position size.
Signal Quality
A 0-100 meta-score reflecting how trustworthy a given Governor run is — based on how many algorithms had real data, how much they agreed, and whether any high-impact overrides fired. Below 60, Claude is instructed to lower confidence.
Signal Conflict
When AURUM's algorithms disagree significantly — a wide spread between bullish and bearish scores, or high standard deviation across results. Flagged as moderate or severe conflict, reducing the Governor's confidence rating.
Smart Money
Institutional investors, hedge funds, and corporate insiders whose positioning often predicts price movement. Options flow and insider transactions are AURUM's two highest-weighted smart money signals in the Governor.
Macro Regime
The current macroeconomic environment — goldilocks, low-rate expansion, high-rate expansion, recession, stagflation, risk-off, deflation risk, or high-volatility bear. The Governor re-weights which algorithms it trusts most based on the detected regime.
Sentiment Velocity
The rate of change in sentiment — not just whether it's positive but whether it's accelerating or decelerating. Accelerating positive sentiment is a stronger signal than peaked sentiment.
Self-Rating
AURUM's system for grading its own accuracy. Picks are logged at entry and automatically compared against the S&P 500's return 30 days later. Runs quietly in the background each time the Admin page loads.
Beat Market
A pick that outperformed the S&P 500 (SPY) return over the same 30-day period. The benchmark used by AURUM's self-rating system and shown on the Report page.
Correlation Risk
When two or more watchlist stocks move almost identically. High correlation means your watchlist provides less diversification than it appears.
Earnings Cycle
The period around a company's quarterly earnings report. The Governor applies a confidence penalty to signals within the earnings window due to elevated binary-event risk.
Options Flow
The volume and direction of options contracts being traded, including sweeps and dark pool activity. Unusual call volume suggests bullish smart money positioning; unusual put volume suggests bearish positioning. AURUM's highest-weighted algorithm (2.2x).
Governor
The 26th algorithm — the master orchestrator. Runs after all 25 others, weighting and synthesizing their outputs into one composite score, verdict, and signal quality rating before Claude ever sees the data.
Paper Trading
Simulated trading with virtual money, tracked entirely in AURUM's own database — no broker or ID required. AURUM's simulator starts with $10,000 in simulated funds.
Degradation Notice
A message included in the Governor's output whenever an algorithm fails, is skipped due to missing data, or a key data source (like options flow) isn't connected. Claude is told exactly what didn't run so it can account for the gap.
Plug and Play
AURUM's architecture for data sources. The integration code is already built for each algorithm — adding an API key (Twelve Data, Finnhub, Unusual Whales, Quiver) activates real data immediately without any additional coding.
